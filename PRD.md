# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Clockivo Rich Blog Posts

## 1. Objective
To establish a clear, repeatable framework for creating high-quality, scientifically-backed, and SEO-optimized blog posts for Clockivo. These posts must stand out from generic AI-generated content by being human-centric, factual, engaging, and seamlessly integrated with Clockivo's core tools (Online Alarm Clock, Pomodoro Timer).

## 2. Core Requirements

### 2.1 Keyword Strategy (SEO)
- **Short-Tail Keywords:** Must include high-volume keywords such as "Online Alarm Clock", "Pomodoro Timer", "Study Timer", "Time Management".
- **Long-Tail Keywords:** Must include conversational, intent-driven phrases (e.g., "how to focus on studying for long hours", "best scientifically proven time management techniques", "why am I always tired in the morning").
- **Placement:** Keywords should appear naturally in the H1, H2s, the first 100 words, and the conclusion. No keyword stuffing.

### 2.2 Tone and Style (Human-Written Feel)
- **Authenticity:** The tone must be conversational, empathetic, and relatable. 
- **Banned AI Jargon:** Strictly avoid generic AI transition phrases such as "In conclusion", "Let's delve into", "It's important to note", "In today's fast-paced world", or "Furthermore".
- **Formatting:** Use short paragraphs (max 3-4 sentences), bold text for emphasis, bullet points, and blockquotes for readability.

### 2.3 Scientific Proofs & Data
- **Credibility:** Every major claim about productivity, sleep, or focus MUST be backed by scientific references or psychological principles (e.g., Zeigarnik Effect, Ultradian Rhythms, Circadian Rhythm, Dopamine reward loops).
- **Explanation:** Explain *why* a technique works biologically or psychologically, rather than just telling the user to do it.

### 2.4 Internal Linking Strategy
- **Contextual Hooks:** Seamlessly weave links to Clockivo's features within the narrative.
- **Targets:**
  - Link to `/alarm-clock` when discussing sleep cycles, waking up early, or morning routines.
  - Link to `/` (home) or specific timer tools when discussing deep work, Pomodoro sessions, or time tracking.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** Include a natural CTA at the end of the post encouraging users to try Clockivo's tools.

### 2.5 Structure of a Post
1. **Engaging Hook:** Start with a relatable problem (e.g., procrastination, brain fog).
2. **The Science/Root Cause:** Explain the psychology behind the problem.
3. **Actionable Solutions:** Provide practical steps, including the use of timers or alarms.
4. **Internal Link Integration:** "This is why we built Clockivo..."
5. **Conclusion:** A short, motivating sign-off.

## 3. Success Metrics
- Increased organic traffic for long-tail keywords.
- Longer Average Time on Page (indicating high readability).
- Higher Click-Through Rate (CTR) to Clockivo's core tools (`/alarm-clock` and Timer).
